ABOUT ME

HEY, I’M CAILIN :)

I’m fascinated by the little things that make an experience memorable: an unexpected interaction, a cohesive visual system, a clever piece of motion, or a detail you might not notice until you look twice. I enjoy working across mediums and figuring out how visuals, storytelling, and interaction can work together to create something that feels deeply intentional.

When I’m not staring at a computer screen, I’m usually exploring: hiking, going to concerts, birdwatching, wandering through museums, or finding something new to be curious about. My Art History and Advertising & PR minors have only added to that curiosity, giving me even more ways to think about how people see and connect to the world around them.
